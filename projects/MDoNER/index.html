Why this exists

Reactive response can't keep up with monsoon terrain

Heavy rainfall, fragile slopes and fast-expanding infrastructure make the North Eastern Region one of the most landslide-exposed parts of the country. Roads get cut, villages get isolated, and responders find out after the ground has already moved.

MDoNER exists to move that moment earlier — from clearing debris to predicting the slope that's about to fail, and telling the people standing on it.

System modules

Five parts, one pipeline

From raw signal to a warning someone can act on — each module hands off to the next.

01
IngestionMulti-source data ingestion
Continuously synchronises the signals a slope actually depends on.
  • Rainfall & forecasts via IMD APIs
  • Terrain, slope & imagery via NESAC / ISRO's Landslide Atlas
  • Soil moisture & inclinometer readings from field IoT sensors
  • Historical landslide inventories for model training
02
PredictionAI/ML predictive engine
Turns incoming signal into a score and a countdown.
  • Dynamic susceptibility score — Low, Moderate, High, Severe — per geolocation
  • Time-to-failure estimate, up to a 3-hour lead window
03
Field inputCitizen crowdsourcing app
Puts eyes on the ground where sensors can't reach.
  • Geo-tagged photo/video reporting of cracks and slope movement
  • Offline-first: saves locally, syncs when signal returns
04
VisualisationInteractive GIS dashboard
Gives district authorities one map to make decisions from.
  • Real-time risk heatmaps over topographic layers
  • Critical roads, bridges and villages overlaid to flag connectivity threats
05
ResponseAutomated alert generation
Closes the loop from breached threshold to warned resident.
  • Push, SMS and WhatsApp alerts on threshold breach
  • Delivered in Assamese, Khasi, Mizo, Bengali, Hindi and more
